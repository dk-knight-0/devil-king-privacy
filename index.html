4. Your privacy rights

For users in European Economic Area / United Kingdom (GDPR):

When you launch the Game, the Google UMP consent form will pop‑up. You can accept or refuse personalized advertising tracking. You may revisit and modify your consent choices anytime from the Privacy Settings button within the Game, which will open your system‑external browser to display relevant content.

You can also reset your advertising identifier via your Android system settings to limit personalized ads.
